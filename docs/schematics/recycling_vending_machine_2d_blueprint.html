⚠️
CRITICAL WIRING RULE (Servo Jitter & ESP32 Brownout Prevention):

Do NOT power the two SG90 servos or the ESP32-CAM from the Arduino Uno's onboard 5V pin! The current spikes when motors move will trigger microcontroller brownout resets. Feed power to servos, ESP32, and Arduino Vin directly from the 5V 2A SMPS power supply and keep all GND pins tied together in a common ground.

Timing Sequence:
  1. Item placed on staging tray → IR 1 triggers LOW.
  2. ESP32-CAM captures frame, runs inference (approx. 400ms).
  3. Arduino receives class byte over Serial → commands Servos to target angles.
  4. Holding delay: 2.5 seconds (item slides into bin).
  5. Servos reset to 0° closed position → LCD displays reward and invites next user.
