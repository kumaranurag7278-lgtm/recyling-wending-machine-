Super Simple & Feasible Vending Machine Structure (1 Servo Only)

100% BUILDABLE IN 45 MINS • ZERO SLIDERS • ZERO JAMS
💡 THE SIMPLEST MECHANICAL CONCEPT (No Ramps, No Pipes!)
Forget narrow slides or complex 3-way branching funnels! The easiest, fail-proof way to build this with cardboard and 1 servo is the Rotating Carousel (Turntable) Design:
  • Fixed Drop Hole on Top: A bottle/can drops straight down into ONE fixed spot.
  • Rotating Bottom with 3 Bins: The bottom contains a circular cardboard tray divided into 3 equal 120° pie slices (Plastic, Metal, Paper).
  • Single Servo on the Axis: The servo sits at the center pivot. When AI detects "Plastic", it turns the tray so the Plastic slice is under the drop hole. The item falls straight in. Impossible to jam!
1. Why It Won't Jam (100% Feasible):

There are NO narrow inclined ramps. The item drops vertically by straight gravity directly into the deep compartment. Even a 2-liter bottle or heavy tin can cannot get stuck.

2. How 1 Servo Handles All 3 Materials:

The servo only rotates a balanced flat tray before the item is dropped. The servo doesn't lift or push the bottle!
• Plastic = Servo moves tray to 0°
• Metal = Servo moves tray to 90°
• Paper = Servo moves tray to 180°
