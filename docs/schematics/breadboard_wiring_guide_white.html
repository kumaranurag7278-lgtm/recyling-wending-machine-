PHYSICAL 400-POINT BREADBOARD CONNECTION GUIDE

Special Solution for Arduino Uno SMD Board (Without Pre-soldered Headers)

⚠️ AGAR UNO PAR BLACK FEMALE HEADERS NAHI HAIN TOH KYA KAREIN?

Aapke bill me "Arduino Uno SMD Board" hai. Market me SMD Uno ke saath aksar Berg Strip / Male Header Pins ka ek chhota packet aata hai ya sirf empty through-holes hote hain. Isko breadboard se jodne ke 2 sabse aasan tareeqe hain:

  1. Tareeqa 1 (Sabse Best & Strong): Uno ke packet me aayi black female header strip (ya male pin strip) ko Uno board ke holes me daal kar soldering iron se 2 minute me solder kar dein.
  2. Tareeqa 2 (Bina Soldering ke): Aapke paas Male-to-Female (M-F) jumper cables hain. Jumper wire ka Male pin seedhe Uno ke hole ke andar daalo aur piche se halka sa tape ya glue lagao taaki hil na sake, aur uska doosra Female/Male end Breadboard me laga do.

Kadam-dar-Kadam (Step-by-Step) Kaise Connect Karein:

  1. Step 1: Power Bus Setup: 5V 2A SMPS ke DC barrel jack ka Red wire breadboard ki Top Red line (+) me daalo, aur Black wire breadboard ki Top Blue line (-) me daalo. Ab poore breadboard ko 5V power mil gayi.
  2. Step 2: Uno Board ko Power dena: Ek wire breadboard ke (+) se nikaal kar Uno ke 5V hole me daalo, aur doosra wire (-) se nikaal kar Uno ke GND hole me daalo.
  3. Step 3: Servos ko direct power dena: Dono SG90 servos ke Red wires breadboard ke (+) me aur Brown wires (-) me daalo. Servo 1 ka Orange wire Uno ke D9 hole me, aur Servo 2 ka Orange wire Uno ke D10 hole me daalo.
  4. Step 4: ESP32-CAM jodhna: ESP32 ka 5V pin breadboard ke (+) me, GND pin (-) me. ESP32 ka U0T (TX) wire Uno ke D4 hole me, aur U0R (RX) wire 1k resistor lagakar Uno ke D5 hole me daalo.
  5. Step 5: IR Sensor & LCD jodhna: IR sensor ka VCC/GND breadboard ke (+)/(-) me aur OUT wire Uno ke D2 hole me. 16x2 LCD ka VCC/GND (+)/(-) me aur SDA wire Uno ke A4 me, SCL wire Uno ke A5 me daalo.
