🔧 Correction 1: Typo "A33" Fixed

In your reference image, SCL was mistakenly written as A33 SCL. Arduino Uno has no pin A33. We corrected it to Pin A5 (SCL) and Pin A4 (SDA).

🎯 Correction 2: 1 IR Sensor Optimized

Since you have 1 IR sensor, it is wired to Pin D2 (Intake). The exit chute detection is handled by a 2.5s non-blocking timer in code.

⚡ Correction 3: 3.3V Logic Safe

Arduino TX (5V) connects to ESP32 RX (3.3V) through a simple 1kΩ / 2kΩ resistor divider from your kit to protect the ESP32-CAM chip from voltage damage.
